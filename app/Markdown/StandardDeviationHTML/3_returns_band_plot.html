What you are seeing: Each point shows the stock’s daily percent change. Positive values mean the price increased since the previous day. Negative values mean it decreased.

What the shaded band represents: The shaded region shows one standard deviation above and below the average return. This range represents the stock’s typical daily movement during this period.

How to interpret it: Most points should fall within the band. Points outside the band indicate unusually large price changes, which signal higher short-term volatility and increased risk.

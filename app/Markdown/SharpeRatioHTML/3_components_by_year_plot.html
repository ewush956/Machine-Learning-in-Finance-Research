What you are seeing: For each calendar year, bars show annualized excess return and annualized volatility, while the line shows that year’s Sharpe ratio.

Why this is Sharpe-specific: Sharpe ratio is the direct relationship between excess return and volatility. This chart decomposes the ratio into its two ingredients so you can see why Sharpe improved or worsened.

How to interpret it: Sharpe rises when excess return increases faster than volatility. If volatility climbs without enough extra return, Sharpe typically declines.
